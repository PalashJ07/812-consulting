Phase details

Select a phase to see its format and preparation guidance.

01 ApplicationYour introduction to 812
What to expect

Submit your application and resume for review. Optional coffee chats take place August 29–30, and first-round decisions are released August 31.

Process
Application→Resume Review→Optional Coffee Chat
02 Group InterviewHow you contribute to a team
What we assess

How you collaborate, communicate, think critically, and move a team discussion forward—not simply how often you speak. Second-round decisions are released September 2.

Format
Introduction→Case 1→Case 2
Strong candidates
  • Offer insightful, well-reasoned ideas
  • Communicate clearly and professionally
  • Invite others in and elevate the group
03 Individual InterviewYour experiences, motivation, and thinking
What we assess

Your communication, professionalism, motivation, critical thinking, and overall fit with 812.

Format
TMAY→Behavioral→Case→Your Questions
Strong candidates
  • Tell structured, authentic stories
  • Show specific enthusiasm for 812
  • Approach the case clearly and analytically
04 Social InterviewA more relaxed conversation
What to expect

A relaxed environment where we can learn more about your personality, communication style, teamwork, and engagement beyond traditional interviews.

Format
Rotating Stations
05 DecisionFinal review and team fit
How decisions work

Candidates are evaluated after every phase. Final selections reflect performance across the process, available project spots, and team needs.
