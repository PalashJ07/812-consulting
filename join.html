Recruitment Process

Five phases designed to understand how you think, communicate, and contribute.

No prior consulting experience is required. We value preparation, genuine interest in 812, thoughtful problem-solving, and the ability to make a team better.

  1. 01

    Phase 1

    Application

    Submit your application and resume. Coffee chats are optional, but highly encouraged for learning more about 812.

    ProcessApplication → Resume Review → Optional Coffee Chat
  2. 02

    Phase 2

    Group Interview

    We evaluate how you collaborate, communicate, think critically, and improve a team discussion—not simply how often you speak.

    FormatIntroduction → Case 1 → Case 2
    • Insightful, well-reasoned ideas
    • Clear and professional communication
    • Natural collaboration that elevates others
  3. 03

    Phase 3

    Individual Interview

    A conversation focused on your experiences, motivation, professionalism, critical thinking, and fit with 812.

    FormatTell Me About Yourself → Behavioral Questions → Case → Your Questions
    • Structured, authentic storytelling
    • Specific motivation and enthusiasm for 812
    • Clear, analytical problem-solving
  4. 04

    Phase 4

    Social Interview

    A more relaxed setting to understand your personality, communication style, teamwork, and engagement beyond traditional interviews.

    FormatRotating Stations
  5. 05

    Phase 5

    Decision

    Candidates are evaluated after every phase. Final selections reflect performance across the process, available project spots, and team needs.
